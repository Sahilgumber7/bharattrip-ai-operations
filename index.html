Target Process: 5-Stage Deterministic Lifecycle

Ticket created instantly from Portal, Email, or WhatsApp. Canonical RF-ID issued.

Stage 3
FINANCE_QUEUED

Automated database push to Finance queue. Zero tickets dropped between teams.

Auto-batch approval for standard tickets ≤ INR 25k. Eliminates single-manager choke.

Stage 5
SETTLED / DECLINED

Funds disbursed. Automated notification sent with bank reference & fee breakdown.
